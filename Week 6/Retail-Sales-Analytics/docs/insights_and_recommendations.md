# Business Insights & Recommendations

## Five or more insights
1. **Scale:** Total sales are 480,580.26 generic currency units across 1,190 distinct orders. This is the baseline for KPI monitoring.
2. **Profitability:** Total profit is 178,868.70, corresponding to an aggregate profit margin of 37.22%. Use margin alongside sales; high revenue alone does not guarantee high profitability.
3. **Category mix:** Furniture is the highest-sales category in this sample. Check whether it also leads in profit and margin before allocating additional budget.
4. **Geographic mix:** North has the highest sales among regions in this sample. Normalize by store count, campaign spend, or customer base before judging operational efficiency.
5. **Product concentration:** Desk is the highest-sales product. Review its inventory availability and margin, and monitor whether sales concentration creates supply risk.
6. **Margin variation:** Office Supplies has the highest aggregate category margin, while Technology has the lowest. Investigate pricing, costs, returns, and discounting.
7. **Seasonality:** 2024-01 is the highest-sales month and 2024-12 is the lowest-sales month in this generated sample. Verify whether a comparable seasonal pattern exists in real historical data.

## Data-driven recommendations
1. **Inventory and campaign planning:** Use month-by-category and region visuals to plan replenishment and campaigns, but validate with real inventory, stockout, and marketing-spend data.
2. **Margin-aware discounting:** Track profit margin and average discount together. Test promotions on a controlled basis and assess incremental profit, not revenue alone.
3. **Product portfolio review:** Pair product sales with gross margin, returns, and holding cost before changing assortment.
4. **Performance normalization:** Compare regions using sales per store, per customer, and per unit of marketing spend when those fields become available.

## Limitation
All numeric insights above come from a synthetic dataset created for demonstration. Do not present them as real business findings.
